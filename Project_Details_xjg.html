血痂谷

Crust‑Blood Valley

Sealed off from the outside world, Scab Valley is plagued by a mysterious endemic disease. Players take on the role of an experimentally modified Chimera. By concocting special potions through the Dream Machine, you gradually regain your human form. Through repeated transformations and metamorphoses, you uncover the buried truth of your origin.

Chinese Wasteland Sci-Fi Point-and-Click Puzzle Item Crafting AVG Codex Collection Eastern Aesthetics Late‑Qing Branching Storylines & Multiple Endings

System Breakdown

Core Gameplay Loop

+

New Game / Continue Game → Explore indoor and outdoor areas of Scab Valley → Collect items → Craft potions → Drink potions to trigger metamorphosis → Trigger key story beats → Ending evaluation

First Run: First encounter with Zhuge Lan → Trigger the Little Girl event (hard-locked cutscene) → Unlock contextual dialogues at each node → Achieve the initial ending → Zhuge Lan’s Resolution

Late Playthroughs (2+ Runs): Zhuge Lan appears with a wheelchair → The Little Girl event loops infinitely with variant versions across different routes → Multi-node branching dialogues → Story convergence leading to final endings

Special Trigger Node: After the 30th potion consumption — regardless of potion type, all default node storylines are fully overwritten by exclusive alternate content. Zhuge Lan’s hidden belongings are exposed, revealing a mysterious viral-blocking injection. After Aunt Hong administers the injection to the little girl, she unexpectedly achieves a complete recovery, birthing a miracle ending defined by negative negation yields positive.

Synthesis System

+

Five Base Materials:
A. Broken Machine Inner‑Casing (Salvaged from scrapped machinery using a screwdriver)
B. Crystal Orb (Found on indoor desk)
C. Machine Oil (Beside the desk)
D. Rainwater (Collected from outdoor puddles using a vase)
E. Inextinguishable Eerie Flame (Obtained by interacting with the eternal outdoor fire using bamboo)

Metamorphosis Stages:
Viscus Stage (1‑6): Six basic combinations: AB, AC, AE, BC, BD, BE
Blood Stage (1‑9): Introduces a second secondary material, corresponding to more complex visceral forms
Bone Stage (1‑9): Form leans toward skeletal structures
Figure Stage (1‑9): Form leans toward humanoid or bestial shapes
Face Stage (Final): Final forms including Human (Yuanjun), Sealion, Jiangtun, and others

Root‑Tree Mechanic: Recipe progress carries over within the Root‑Tree. On the N‑th playthrough, players may skip previously explored paths to directly craft compounds. The protagonist has a limited bodily load capacity and cannot keep consuming potions indefinitely.

Codex System

+

Unlock Conditions: The notebook‑skin codex unlocks after the first encounter with Zhuge Lan; the wooden‑plank‑skin codex unlocks upon completion of the first playthrough.

Notebook Skin: Zhuge Lan uses a Polaroid camera to take photos documenting the protagonist’s forms. The notebook sits on the windowsill and can be viewed at any time. It contains five pages, with one new entry added after each metamorphosis. Once Zhuge Lan departs, the hand icon in the top‑left corner disappears.

Wooden Board Skin: The NPC interaction window is redesigned with a door curtain style. Players can enter the codex interface by tapping the codex pattern on the door. The wooden board can be pushed upward to reveal the NPC interaction window. When flipping pages, codex content may appear on either the left or right side of the screen.

Potion Marking Function:
Players can drag potion bottles to the dashed areas on corresponding codex pages for temporary marking, which displays "Name?". When a potion correctly matches and successfully triggers a metamorphosis, a confirmation animation will play, featuring flickering thin wing effects, erasure of the question mark, and a checkmark confirmation icon.

Problems & Future Improvements

  • Lacking prior experience in collaborative team‑based projects, major flaws emerged in project management. Development productivity was low, and miscommunication frequently occurred, as team members struggled to articulate ideas accurately.
  • Severe creative‑concept disagreements arose with the lead writer, halting further project progress. A potential restart will be considered only if future circumstances permit purchasing the copyright for the lead writer’s creative contributions.
